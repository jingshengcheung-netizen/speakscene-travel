# 景点购票 / Attraction tickets

- **id:** `tickets`
- **level:** A2-B1
- **goal:** Buy tickets, ask opening hours, student/senior price, photo policy.

## Starter (coach opens)
> Hello. Tickets for today? How many adults?

## Rubric focus
Adult/child, combo ticket, last entry, can I take photos.

## Session checklist
- [ ] Learner states need clearly
- [ ] Uses at least 3 scene-relevant phrases
- [ ] Can rewrite one awkward turn into natural English
- [ ] Ends with a polite closing line
