# 问路指路 / Asking for directions

- **id:** `directions`
- **level:** A2-B1
- **goal:** Ask how to get somewhere and confirm landmarks / transit.

## Starter (coach opens)
> Excuse me—you look a bit lost. Where are you trying to go?

## Rubric focus
How do I get to..., left/right, straight, how long, which bus.

## Session checklist
- [ ] Learner states need clearly
- [ ] Uses at least 3 scene-relevant phrases
- [ ] Can rewrite one awkward turn into natural English
- [ ] Ends with a polite closing line
