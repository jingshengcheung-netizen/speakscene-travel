# 机场值机 / Airport check-in

- **id:** `airport-checkin`
- **level:** A2-B1
- **goal:** Check in for a flight, choose seat, ask about baggage allowance.

## Starter (coach opens)
> Good morning. May I see your passport and booking confirmation, please?

## Rubric focus
Passport, booking ref, window/aisle, baggage weight, gate.

## Session checklist
- [ ] Learner states need clearly
- [ ] Uses at least 3 scene-relevant phrases
- [ ] Can rewrite one awkward turn into natural English
- [ ] Ends with a polite closing line
