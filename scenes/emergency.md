# 紧急求助 / Emergency help

- **id:** `emergency`
- **level:** A2-B1
- **goal:** Ask for help calmly: lost phone, feel unwell, need police/embassy.

## Starter (coach opens)
> Are you okay? Do you need help?

## Rubric focus
I lost my..., please call..., embassy, I don't feel well.

## Session checklist
- [ ] Learner states need clearly
- [ ] Uses at least 3 scene-relevant phrases
- [ ] Can rewrite one awkward turn into natural English
- [ ] Ends with a polite closing line
