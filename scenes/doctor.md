# 看医生 / Seeing a doctor

- **id:** `doctor`
- **level:** A2-B1
- **goal:** Describe symptoms clearly and ask what to do next.

## Starter (coach opens)
> Hello, I'm Dr. Lee. What brings you in today?

## Rubric focus
I've had..., fever, pain scale, allergy to medicine, prescription.

## Session checklist
- [ ] Learner states need clearly
- [ ] Uses at least 3 scene-relevant phrases
- [ ] Can rewrite one awkward turn into natural English
- [ ] Ends with a polite closing line
