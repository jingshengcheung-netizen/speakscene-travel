# 酒店入住 / 投诉 / Hotel check-in / complaint

- **id:** `hotel`
- **level:** A2-B1
- **goal:** Check in, ask for Wi‑Fi, then politely report a room issue.

## Starter (coach opens)
> Welcome. Do you have a reservation under your name?

## Rubric focus
Reservation, late checkout, Wi-Fi password, AC not working.

## Session checklist
- [ ] Learner states need clearly
- [ ] Uses at least 3 scene-relevant phrases
- [ ] Can rewrite one awkward turn into natural English
- [ ] Ends with a polite closing line
