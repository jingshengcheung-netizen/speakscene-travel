# 打车 / 网约车 / Taxi / rideshare

- **id:** `taxi`
- **level:** A2-B1
- **goal:** Give destination, confirm fare/route, ask to stop.

## Starter (coach opens)
> Hi, where to?

## Rubric focus
Take me to..., via..., how much, keep the change, pull over.

## Session checklist
- [ ] Learner states need clearly
- [ ] Uses at least 3 scene-relevant phrases
- [ ] Can rewrite one awkward turn into natural English
- [ ] Ends with a polite closing line
