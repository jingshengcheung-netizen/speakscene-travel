# 购物 / 议价 / Shopping / bargaining

- **id:** `shopping`
- **level:** A2-B1
- **goal:** Ask size/price, try on, negotiate politely, pay.

## Starter (coach opens)
> Hi! Looking for anything in particular today?

## Rubric focus
Do you have this in..., too expensive, any discount, I'll take it.

## Session checklist
- [ ] Learner states need clearly
- [ ] Uses at least 3 scene-relevant phrases
- [ ] Can rewrite one awkward turn into natural English
- [ ] Ends with a polite closing line
